OpenFaceID (SightLock)

Open-source face recognition for your desktop.
Private by design. Runs locally. No cloud account required.

Important Security Boundary:

  • Your camera video feed stays strictly on your physical device.
  • All biometric detection and recognition occurs locally in volatile RAM.
  • OpenFaceID does NOT replace your operating system login password.

Privacy Disclosure

Camera: Used for live face detection and presence monitoring.
Biometric Data: Mathematical face representations are stored locally when enrollment is enabled.
Network: Recognition does not require any cloud service or network transmission.
Storage: Identity data is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using keys from your OS keystore.
Control: You can pause camera processing at any time from the menu bar or UI.
