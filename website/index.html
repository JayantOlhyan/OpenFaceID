Seamless Installation

1

Download DMG

Download the official OpenFaceID-0.2.1-rc.1-arm64.dmg release image.

2

Drag to Applications

Open the DMG and drag the OpenFaceID.app icon into your Applications folder.

3

Grant Camera Permission

On first launch, grant the macOS TCC camera permission when prompted. Hardware capture activates immediately.

4

Enroll & Secure

Complete the guided 5-pose face enrollment in seconds. Your biometric data never leaves volatile RAM.
